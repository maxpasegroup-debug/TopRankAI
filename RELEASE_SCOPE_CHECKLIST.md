## 1. Frozen release boundary

- Exams: JEE Main and NEET UG only. Canonical codes are JEE_MAIN and NEET.
- Markets: India and UAE only; do not interpret "Middle East" as global approval.
- Audience: students of all ages, using the approved actual age/country/guardian
  workflow. No blanket adults-only rule. Public non-personal exploration is
  distinct from permission to register or process student data.
- Offer: free invited pilot first, followed by a separately approved gradual free
  public release. No card collection, checkout, subscription or payment dependency.
- Experience: goal -> daily mission -> lesson -> practice -> revision -> mock ->
  evidence-based progress -> next available action, supported by deterministic SIA.
- Launch language: English for the approved content subset. Additional languages
  require their own reviewed content and acceptance; do not advertise translation.
- Guardian registration authorization is required where applicable. Optional
  progress sharing remains separate and does not enable intrusive default controls.
- Product scope is frozen here; cohort size, content inventory, legal approval,
  deployment target and authentication activation are NOT approved by this file.
